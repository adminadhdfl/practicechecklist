                            🛑 Data Isolation Protocol
                        

                        
    
                            
  • NO Mass Migration: Private practice patient records CANNOT be imported into the doctor's new employed practice EHR without individually signed HIPAA authorizations.
    • 
                            
  • EHR Export Format: Export charts as searchable PDFs/CCDA files including treatment plans, progress notes, billing history, and audit trails.
    • 
                            
  • Vault Storage: Store exported charts in an encrypted HIPAA-compliant cloud storage (e.g., Encrypted AWS S3, HIPAA-compliant Google Workspace with BAA).
    • 
                        

                    


                    

                        

                            📞 Custodian of Records Setup
                        

                        
    
                            
  • Designation: Formally name a Custodian of Records (or record archive service) responsible for answering future legal/subpoena requests.
    • 
                            
  • Contact Information: Ensure the records email and phone number remain active for at least 3 years post-closure.
    • 
                            
  • Medical Board Notification: Check state medical board requirements regarding submitting an official practice closure affidavit.
